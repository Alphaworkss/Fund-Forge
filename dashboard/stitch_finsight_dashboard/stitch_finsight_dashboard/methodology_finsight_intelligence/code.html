analytics How Forecasting Works

Our system treats financial forecasting as a multifaceted time-series problem. Rather than relying on a single algorithm, we aggregate signals across multiple architectures. We analyze historical price action, order book depth, and macroeconomic indicators, feeding this high-dimensional data into our models to project future state probabilities.

hdr_strong Why a Price Range?

Markets are inherently stochastic. Predicting an exact future price is computationally and practically impossible. Instead, we generate a Cone of Uncertainty. By calculating the 70% and 95% confidence intervals, we provide a realistic band where the asset is statistically likely to trade, visualizing volatility and risk rather than false certainty.

The Engine: AI Models

Statistical Base show_chart

ARIMA

AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average. Provides our foundational baseline by analyzing standard historical trends, seasonality, and mean-reversion characteristics in stationary data.

Gradient Boosting account_tree

XGBoost

Excels at handling tabular data and non-linear relationships. We use XGBoost to interpret complex interactions between varied macroeconomic indicators and price movements.

Transformer psychology

PatchTST

A state-of-the-art time-series transformer model. By patching time series data, it captures long-term dependencies and subtle contextual patterns that traditional models miss.

Walk-Forward Validation

To prevent overfitting and ensure real-world accuracy, we employ strict Walk-Forward Validation. Instead of standard cross-validation which leaks future data, we train models on a sliding historical window and test them exclusively on unseen subsequent periods.
